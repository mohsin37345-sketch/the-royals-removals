Moving in Wythall: What You Need to Know

Moving in Wythall means planning around 3 things: narrow village lanes, the Alcester Road (A435), and traffic at M42 junction 3. The village sits about 7 miles (11 km) south of Birmingham city centre, on the border with Solihull, so many moves run north into Birmingham or east into Shirley and Solihull.

We plan the route and the arrival time around rush hour on the A435 and the M42. Lanes near Tanner's Green and Inkford are tight for large vans, so we check access at your address before the day and send the right size of vehicle.

Moving into central Birmingham? Our vans are compliant with the Birmingham Clean Air Zone (CAZ), which covers the roads inside the A4540 Middleway. You pay no CAZ charge on our vehicles.

Fully Insured House Removals in Wythall

Fully insured removals in Wythall come with 2 types of cover: Goods in Transit (GIT) up to £50,000 and public liability up to £5 million.

GIT insurance protects your belongings against accidental damage, theft or loss while they are being moved. Public liability insurance covers injury or property damage during your move.

Items you pack yourself and items in storage are not covered by the GIT policy. Enhanced cover is available for high-value belongings.
