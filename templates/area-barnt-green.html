Planning a Move in Barnt Green

A move in Barnt Green depends on 3 things: village centre parking, access on rural lanes, and the route to the M5, M42 or A38. Barnt Green lies around 10 miles (16 km) south of Birmingham city centre. The Lickey Hills sit on one side and the Bittell Reservoirs on the other.

Living in Barnt Green: Popular Areas

Living in Barnt Green means choosing from 5 parts of the village and its surroundings. The village and civil parish sits in Bromsgrove District, Worcestershire, and counted 2,035 residents in about 850 households at the 2021 census.

  • The village centre: Home to shops, cafés, the Victoria pub and the station.
  • Streets near the Lickey Hills: The wooded hills run along the parish's north-west edge.
  • The Bittell Reservoirs side: The Upper and Lower Bittell Reservoirs lie to the east, built in the 1820s to supply the Worcester and Birmingham Canal.
  • The conservation area: Narrow roads run through parts of this protected area.
  • Cofton Hackett: A neighbouring village close to Barnt Green.

The unstaffed station serves the Cross-City Line and the Birmingham to Worcester line, roughly 9 miles (14 km) by rail from Birmingham New Street.

From the station exit, a path climbs to the Lickey Hills.
