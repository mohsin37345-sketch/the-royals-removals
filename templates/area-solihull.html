Moving in Solihull: What You Need to Know

Moving in Solihull means planning around 3 things: the M42 junctions, parking in the town centre and at apartment blocks, and the council that covers your address. Solihull sits about 7.5 miles (12 km) south-east of Birmingham city centre, with the A41 Warwick Road and the A34 Stratford Road as its main routes.

The M42 splits the borough. Junction 5 serves Solihull town centre, and junction 6 serves the National Exhibition Centre (NEC) and Birmingham Airport. We plan the route and arrival time around both.

Solihull is under Solihull Metropolitan Borough Council, not Birmingham City Council. Tell us about parking, driveways, lifts and stairs when you ask for a quote, and the quote is built on that access.

Moving into central Birmingham? Solihull is outside the Birmingham Clean Air Zone (CAZ), which covers the roads inside the A4540 Middleway. Our vans are CAZ compliant, so you pay no CAZ charge on our vehicles at either end.

Parking and Completion Day in Solihull

Parking and completion are the 2 parts of a Solihull move that depend on other people, so both are planned at the quote stage.

Parking for the Removal Van

Permission to park the removal van in Solihull comes from Solihull Council. A dispensation issued by Birmingham City Council is not valid on a Solihull road, so ask Solihull Council about your street.

Town-centre roads near Touchwood, and apartment blocks with shared car parks, leave little space to load. Share parking and lift details in your quote request, so the crew knows where the van will wait.

If Your Completion Is Delayed

A delayed completion means updates from the crew by phone or WhatsApp through the wait. A chain can stall on the day for reasons outside your control.
