Moving in Walmley: What You Need to Know

Moving in Walmley means planning around 3 things: parking at your address, the 20 mph (32 km/h) limit through Walmley Village, and the route to your new home. Walmley sits on the north-eastern edge of Birmingham, in the Royal Town of Sutton Coldfield, so most moves start under Birmingham City Council.

Many Walmley moves run west along Wylde Green Road, through New Hall Valley, into Wylde Green and Erdington. Tell us about parking, driveways and stairs when you ask for a quote, and the quote is built on that access.
